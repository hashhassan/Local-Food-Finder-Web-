Food By Area

Discover the best food spots in different areas of Rawalpindi and Islamabad

Each area in Rawalpindi and Islamabad has its own food culture and specialties. This guide helps you find the best food depending on where you are or where you plan to visit.

📍

Saddar, Rawalpindi

Saddar is one of the busiest areas in Rawalpindi and is famous for its street food culture. You will find BBQ stalls, sweet shops, and chaat corners open till late at night.

Best For:   Seekh Kebab,   Rabri Kheer,   Gol Gappay

Bhabra Bazaar, Rawalpindi

Bhabra Bazaar is the heart of traditional breakfast culture in Rawalpindi. Famous breakfast spots here open as early as 5 AM and serve hundreds of customers every morning.

Best For:   Paye Nihari,   Halwa Puri,   Channay

Raja Bazaar, Rawalpindi

Raja Bazaar is known for its affordable and tasty street food. It is one of the oldest markets in Rawalpindi and has many small food stalls serving local favourites.

Best For:   Chapli Kebab,   Dahi Bhalle,   Samosa

Bahria Town, Rawalpindi

Bahria Town has a growing food scene with many restaurants and cafes. It is popular for family dining and has options ranging from desi food to fast food and desserts.

Best For:   Dahi Bhalle,   Karahi,   BBQ

Murree Road, Rawalpindi

Murree Road is lined with dhabas and restaurants serving hearty meals. It is especially known for rice dishes and whole fried chicken which attract customers throughout the day.

Best For:   Beef Pulao,   Lahori Chargha,   Karahi

F-10 Markaz, Islamabad

F-10 Markaz is one of the most popular food destinations in Islamabad. It has many well known karahi restaurants that stay open late and are perfect for family dinners.
